Paño vs. the Recycle Bin

Paño is the cat who guides visitors around Phill's desktop. Try dragging him onto the Recycle Bin:

  1. Over the bin, his eyelids drop and he looks unimpressed.
  2. Let go and the bin spits him out. He sits down beside it.
  3. He licks his paw and tells you what he thinks.

Paño is sitting on the desktop, minding his business.

Why this helps recruiters

It rewards a curious click with personality instead of a dead end. It also shows how Phill thinks as a Product Manager: plan for what users actually do, including the things they "shouldn't," and turn that edge case into a moment people remember and mention.
